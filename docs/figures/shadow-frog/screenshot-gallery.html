Verdict donut  |  SWE-Bench bug hunting (50 tasks)
Verdict donut — blind bug hunting on 50 SWE-Bench Verified tasks. The LLM judge sorts each task into one of four tiers, labeled by what they actually mean (rather than the §3A code names IDENTIFIED / ADJACENT / AREA / MISSED). The aggregated “Judge ≥ AREA” headline of 88% is the top three slices combined; the strict-tier headline of 22% is the red slice alone.

Figure 5 — Where the +4.7 ppts comes from. Left: per-task outcomes on 50 SWE-Bench Verified tasks (6 Shadow-Frog wins, 2 baseline wins, 1 tie, 7 both-fail, 34 both-pass). Right: hint-delivery funnel: 50 tasks → 23 with hints delivered → 12 opened → 2 judged relevant → 1 actually changes the outcome.
